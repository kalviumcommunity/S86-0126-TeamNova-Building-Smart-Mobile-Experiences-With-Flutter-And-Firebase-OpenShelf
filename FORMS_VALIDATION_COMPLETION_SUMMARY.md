## Comparison: Form Validation Approaches

### Approach 1: Simple TextField
- ❌ No validation
- ❌ Manual error handling
- ❌ Poor UX
- ✅ Simple to implement
- ❌ Not production-ready

### Approach 2: Form + TextFormField
- ✅ Built-in validation
- ✅ Error display
- ✅ Form state management
- ✅ Production-ready
- ✅ This module covers this

### Approach 3: Custom Validation Library
- ✅ Advanced features
- ✅ Highly customizable
- ❌ Extra dependencies
- ❌ Learning curve
- ⚠️ May be overkill

**Recommendation**: Use Approach 2 (TextFormField) for most apps
