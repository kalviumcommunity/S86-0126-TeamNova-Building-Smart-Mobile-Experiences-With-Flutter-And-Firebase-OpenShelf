## Comparison: Provider vs Riverpod

### Provider: Best For
- ✅ Beginners learning state management
- ✅ Small to medium applications
- ✅ Teams comfortable with OOP
- ✅ Simple state patterns
- ✅ Rapid prototyping
- ✅ Maximum community resources

### Riverpod: Best For
- ✅ Large, complex applications
- ✅ Type safety critical
- ✅ Fine-grained reactivity needed
- ✅ Immutable patterns preferred
- ✅ Advanced state patterns
- ✅ Teams with functional experience
